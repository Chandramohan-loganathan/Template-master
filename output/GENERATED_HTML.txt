<p style="margin:0 0 8px 0; text-align:center; line-height:1.35;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 8px 0; text-align:left; line-height:1.35;"><span style="font-weight:bold">ADMISSION / JOINING FEE AND ANNUAL FEE:</span></p>
<p style="margin:0 0 8px 0; text-align:justify; line-height:1.35;">Admission / Joining Fee is a one-time fee collected from the Cardholders upon activation of the Card and may vary for different Card Variants.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 8px 0; text-align:justify; line-height:1.35;">Annual Fee is a fee collected in advance at the beginning of the second year of the Card.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 8px 0; text-align:justify; line-height:1.35;">Both Joining Fee and Annual fee as mentioned hereunder will be charged to the Principal Cardholders Credit Card account and would be reflected in the monthly Credit Card statement of the month in which it is charged.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 8px 0; text-align:justify; line-height:1.35;">No refund of the fees will be available.</p>
